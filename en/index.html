01Brand Story

Brand Story

Just like Mom —
the wish to do it myself.

Children don't imitate what Mom does
because they want to look prettier.

It's the wish to do it themselves, just like Mom.

cocoheali reimagined a familiar grown-up compact,
filled with skincare
made safely for kids.

We turn the moment of copying Mom
into a child's first Self Care experience.

Self-care, to us, is the joy of doing it yourself.

A face that does it herself wears a different expression than one just copying.

To cocoheali, self-care means
a child discovering
the joy of truly doing it themselves.

We aren't a brand that sells cosmetics.
We're the brand behind a child's first
Self Care experience.

    Brand Voice — Internal Reference

    Words we use,
    and words we don't.

    Words We Use

    Do it myselfSkincareSelf-careSkin barrier HabitGrowthPrideMe do itBeing myself

    The campaign tagline is "Me do it" (Korean: "나도 해볼래"). "Being myself" is used to describe the emotional tone the brand aims for. "Confidence" may also be used in a supporting role, per brand guidelines.

    Words We Avoid

    MakeupCosmeticsDressing up

    Language around "identity" is avoided consistently, both domestically and internationally — see the Rini case study for reference — and replaced with concrete actions and emotions instead.
